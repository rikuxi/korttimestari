Plain ranks (AAJ) do not constrain suits. Cards inside parentheses share a suit: (AK) = AK suited, (AK)(AK) = two suited pairs. Concrete cards also work (AsKsQh — same letter = same suit). A lone rank may be of any suit; (T) forces its own suit. Hold'em: AKs / AKo.

Preflop chart: the top-X % range

Use the slider to choose how large a share of the best hands (weighted by combos) belongs to the range. For Hold'em the range is shown on the familiar 13×13 grid: pairs on the diagonal, suited hands above it and offsuit hands below. In Omaha and Omaha5 there are thousands of hand classes and no equivalent grid, so the range is described with summary statistics.

The range is based on this page's all-in equity ranking for the selected player count (best hand first), not on a solver's GTO strategy: a real opening range also depends on position and playability. Clicking a cell searches for that hand in the table.

The heads-up tables (Hold'em, Omaha) are exact: every board and every opponent hand has been enumerated. The rest were computed with a hybrid method (exact over all boards, Monte Carlo over opponent card removal) and each row shows its standard error. The rank range in parentheses tells which ranks the hand could occupy within the error bounds. The data is licensed under CC BY 4.0 — free to use with attribution (Korttimestari).
